09

Why it looks like this

Dark, on purpose

Every tool photographers already use is dark, because a bright interface changes how you judge an image sitting next to it. This product's output is the only thing on screen that should have colour.

Waiting is a state, not an error

Queued, starting, running, done — each has a colour and a number attached. A spinner would be a lie: the GPU genuinely is off, and saying so is what makes the price possible.

Names, not identifiers

The roster is m_cauz_20s_slim internally and "Bekzod · 22" to everyone else. A person choosing who models their product is casting, not querying.

The result is a fork

Download is one option among three. More variations and same-garment-different-model are the moves a seller actually makes next, so they are buttons rather than a return trip through the studio.

Images are from the working test set — the roster's own 300 photographs are on the pod and swap in directly. Prices are marked [PRICE] where a real number is not known yet. English throughout; Uzbek and Russian follow once the structure is settled, which is why every label here is short enough to translate without breaking the layout.
